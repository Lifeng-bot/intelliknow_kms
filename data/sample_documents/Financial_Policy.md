# Financial Policy Document

## Expense Reimbursement
Employees may be reimbursed for business-related expenses incurred while performing their job duties. All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense and must include:
- Original receipts
- Business purpose description
- Date and location of expense
- Amount and currency

### Travel Expenses
- Airfare: Economy class unless business class is approved in advance
- Accommodation: Up to $150 per night for domestic travel, $200 for international
- Meals: Up to $50 per day for domestic travel, $75 for international
- Ground transportation: Actual cost up to $30 per day

### Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses must be pre-approved and are limited to $100 per person per event.

## Budget Approval Process
All departmental budgets must be approved by the Finance Department. Budget requests should include:
- Detailed breakdown of expenses
- Justification for each expense
- Timeline for expenditure
- Expected return on investment

## Procurement Policy
All purchases over $500 require at least three competitive quotes. Purchases over $5,000 require formal approval from the Finance Department.

## Invoice Processing
Invoices should be submitted to the Finance Department with the following information:
- Vendor name and contact information
- Invoice number and date
- Detailed description of goods or services
- Total amount and payment terms
- Purchase order number (if applicable)

## Financial Reporting
Monthly financial reports are due on the 15th of each month for the previous month. Department managers are responsible for reviewing and approving their department's financial reports.

## Contact Information
For finance-related questions, please contact the Finance Department at finance@company.com or extension 5678.
