# Human Resources Policy Document

## Employee Benefits
Our company offers a comprehensive benefits package to all full-time employees including:
- Health insurance (medical, dental, vision)
- Life insurance
- Retirement savings plan with company match
- Paid time off (vacation, sick leave, holidays)
- Professional development opportunities

## Leave Policies
### Vacation Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid vacation leave per year, accrued monthly. Vacation leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance for approval.

### Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave can be used for personal illness or to care for immediate family members.

### Parental Leave
New parents are eligible for up to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child.

## Performance Management
Performance reviews are conducted annually. Employees will receive feedback from their managers and have the opportunity to discuss career goals and development plans.

## Workplace Conduct
All employees are expected to maintain a professional and respectful work environment. Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.
